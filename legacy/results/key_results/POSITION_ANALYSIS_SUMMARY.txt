======================================================================
KEY FINDING: Opposite biases in RI vs PI
======================================================================

RI (Recall FIRST value):
  - Errors come from LATE positions (recent updates)
  - Demonstrates RECENCY BIAS in interference
  - Recent information overwrites early memories

PI (Recall LAST value):
  - Errors come from EARLY positions (first updates)
  - Demonstrates PRIMACY BIAS in interference
  - Early information persists despite updates

This mirrors the 'Unable to Forget' paper's Figure 5/19 findings,
showing that LLMs exhibit opposite interference patterns for
early vs recent information retrieval.
