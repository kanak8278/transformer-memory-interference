CAUSAL INTERPRETATION
---------------------
The "additive promoter, suppressor still firing" hypothesis (from the prior
suppressor ablation) is upheld AND sharpened:

* The promoter heads identified by attention routing (L30H11, L31H12,
  L31H15, L32H0, L32H3, L32H7, L32H10, L32H14) are CAUSALLY RESPONSIBLE
  for v_last building up at L32 in the LoRA model: ablating them drops
  P(v_last) at L32 from 0.98 to 0.70 (Δ -0.28, the largest single-layer
  effect across any ablation tested).
* Downstream layers (L33+) recover to P(v_last) = 1.0 anyway, indicating
  that L33+ contains REDUNDANT pathways that re-amplify v_last when the
  L30-L32 promoter band is partially ablated.
* This redundancy explains why the final-layer effect appears null. Single
  set ablation is not enough to break the LoRA mechanism — it requires
  ablating either the promoter set + their downstream amplifiers, OR all
  v_last-attending heads in L30-L33 (we used top-8; there are 15 with
  Δ > 0.5).

The mechanism is therefore: LoRA installs a v_last-attending circuit in
L30-L32 (with L32H3, L32H7 as the highest-attribution single heads), and
that signal propagates through L33+ which has built-in redundancy to
saturate v_last to 1.0 by L35.
