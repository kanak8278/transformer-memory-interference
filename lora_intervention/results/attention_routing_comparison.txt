INTERPRETATION
--------------
1) The routing change is SPATIALLY LOCALIZED to layers 24-33, peaking at L31-L33.
   L34-L35 (the final readout) are essentially unchanged.
2) Early-layer attention patterns are nearly identical (small negative deltas)
   — LoRA did not rewire feature extraction.
3) Fifteen heads in L30-L33 went from ~0.08-0.23 P(attend to v_last) to
   0.60-0.86 — a 4-12× increase. These heads are now strongly recency-routing
   under CVQ where they previously were not.

NOTE ON METHODOLOGY
-------------------
attn_from_gen was summed across both K columns (test cat + distractor cat)
rather than restricted to the test cat's column — small dilution effect, but
applied identically to baseline and LoRA so deltas are unaffected. For
publication-grade per-head numbers, re-run extraction restricting to the test
category's rounds only (~5 min code change).
