KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1) At L31, ablating these 5 heads RAISES P(v_last) in BOTH models:
     baseline +0.078, LoRA +0.150
   → These heads still SUPPRESS v_last in the LoRA model (the suppression
     mechanism is NOT removed by LoRA).

2) By L33+, the ablation has zero effect on the LoRA model's output (Δ ≈ 0)
   while baseline's L33 still shows a measurable drop (-0.058).
   → LoRA's downstream promotion saturates at P(v_last) = 1.0 and absorbs
     the perturbation entirely.

3) Baseline L31 normal P(v_last) is 0.021. LoRA L31 normal is 0.276 (13×).
   The "release from suppression" signal at L31 is larger in LoRA because
   there is more underlying v_last activity already present at that depth.

REVISED MECHANISM STORY
-----------------------
LoRA did NOT remove the late-layer suppression mechanism. The same heads
in L26-L30 still suppress v_last at L31 — measurably stronger in the LoRA
model (because there's more signal to suppress). What LoRA added is a
PROMOTION mechanism in L30-L33 (the 15 heads identified in attention
routing) that overwhelms the suppression and drives final P(v_last) to 1.0.

The pretraining failure is therefore not "missing v_last representation"
nor "broken suppression heads" — it is "no upper-layer promoter to win the
tug-of-war." LoRA installs the missing promoter; old suppressors are still
firing but their effect is dwarfed.

CONVERGENCE WITH OTHER SESSION FINDINGS
----------------------------------------
- Probing (K=2/N=5): PI-correctness L33 58% → 94%. Encodes the new promoter signal.
- Logit lens (K=2/N=5,10,50): P(v_last) final 0.25→1.00. Promoter wins late.
- Attention routing (K=2/N=30): 15 heads L30-L33 went from 0.08-0.23 to 0.60-0.86
  P(attend to v_last). Identifies the promoter heads physically.
- Stage 3C (this file): old suppressor heads (L26-L30) still suppress at L31,
  but ablating them no longer matters because promoter saturates by L33.

All four signals point to the same layer band (L24-L33, peak L30-L33) and the
same mechanism: ADDITIVE promotion of v_last, not removal of suppression.

3A AND 3B WERE DEGENERATE
-------------------------
3A (attribution patching) and 3B (paired patching) are designed to find heads
that, when changed, would FIX the model's failure. The LoRA model has no
failures to fix at K=2/N=5 (P(v_last) ≈ 1.0). 3A's gradient saturates to
zero; 3B's "skip if pred==expected" guard skips every trial. Only 3C runs.
This is a fundamental experimental-design issue with the LoRA model at this
operating point — not a bug. Pre-LoRA 3A/3B results (in the baseline file)
identified the suppressor cluster; we use that as the ablation target here.
