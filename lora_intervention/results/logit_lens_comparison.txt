INTERPRETATION
--------------
1) "v_last found then suppressed" replicates in baseline:
   K=2/N=5: P(v_last) peaks at L32 (0.34) then DECAYS to L35 (0.25)
   K=2/N=50: barely surfaces (L32=0.02, L35=0.11) — high-load suppression
2) Post-LoRA: late-layer suppression eliminated. P(v_last) at L32 already
   high (0.62–0.99) and reaches the final layer near saturation (0.90–1.00).
3) RI side: baseline final-layer P(v_first) is only 0.36–0.44 even though
   RI behavioral accuracy is high. The model picks v_first as argmax but
   with low confidence/entropy. LoRA sharpens this to P(v_first)=1.00.

Mechanism (combined with probing finding):
- The v_last representation EXISTS in baseline upper layers (L32) but gets
  attenuated before readout (L35). LoRA doesn't build a new v_last finder —
  it removes the late-layer suppression that was tearing down the correct answer.
- Probing showed the substrate (condition discrimination, RI/PI signal in
  the residual) pre-existed. Logit lens shows the v_last token ALSO pre-existed
  in late layers. The pretraining failure was a calibration / readout failure,
  not a representational one. 18k LoRA examples fix the calibration.
