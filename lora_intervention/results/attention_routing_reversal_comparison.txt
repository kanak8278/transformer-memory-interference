INTERPRETATION
--------------
1) The primary v_last promoter circuit (L30-L33) is regime-invariant — same
   heads, same direction, comparable magnitude in both primacy and reversal.

2) Reversal regime activates 4 additional promoter heads in L27-L29 that
   weren't prominent in primacy (L27H6, L27H1, L27H5, L28H10, L29H4). These
   are likely specific to the high-K (15 keys) routing demand: with 15
   distinct keys, the model needs more upstream filtering before the L30-L33
   promoter circuit takes over.

3) Reversal regime also shows modest activation in L34-L35 (+0.08, +0.08)
   absent in primacy. The full readout in this harder regime spreads
   slightly more across the final layers.

4) Baseline P(attend to v_last) is consistently HIGHER in reversal than in
   primacy (e.g., L33: 0.118 vs 0.068 at primacy). This is consistent with
   the regime characterisation: the base model in reversal is already
   recency-biased; LoRA just amplifies further.

The §7 mechanism story can now claim regime-general validity: the LoRA
recovery uses a single attentional circuit in L30-L33 to drive v_last
into the readout, and the circuit is robust enough to handle both the
primacy and reversal failure modes of the base model.
