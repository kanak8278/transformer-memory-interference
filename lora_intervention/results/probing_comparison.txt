INTERPRETATION
--------------
1) Condition discrimination (RI vs PI as a classifier target on the residual
   stream) is already ~100% from layer 3 in BOTH models. The model has always
   known "which question am I being asked." LoRA does not add this signal.

2) RI-correctness probe: late layers (L30-L33) carry a moderate-to-strong
   signal (77-81%) about whether RI will be answered correctly, even in the
   baseline. LoRA bumps L33 from 81% → 93% — refinement of an existing signal.

3) PI-correctness probe: BASELINE LAYERS L27-L33 ARE AT CHANCE (53-58%) — the
   model does not encode whether it is about to get PI right. POST-LORA: the
   same layers jump to 77-94%. LoRA installs a PI-correctness signal in the
   upper-layer circuitry that previously had none.

Mechanism story for §7:
   The skill was LATENT, not missing. The condition-discrimination machinery
   pre-existed; the late-layer readout circuitry pre-existed; the RI-correctness
   signal pre-existed. What LoRA added was a PI-correctness representation
   in the SAME late layers (L27-L33) — the model already had the substrate,
   it just lacked the trained mapping from condition → correct-readout for PI.
